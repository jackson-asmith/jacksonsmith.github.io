## How I approach engineering

* **Reliability is a feature** — systems shouldn't need heroics to operate.
* **Automation must reduce risk, not create it** — automating a bad process just produces bad outcomes faster.
* **Modernize incrementally** — improve what exists first; rewrite only when the evidence supports it.
* **Governance should enable engineers** — make the compliant path the easiest path.
* **Testing comes before trust** — confidence should come from evidence, not familiarity.
* **Documentation is engineering work** — if the next engineer can't operate it, it isn't finished.
